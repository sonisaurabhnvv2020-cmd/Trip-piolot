### 2. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: DepartWise AI empowers commuters, executives, and international travelers to register planned itineraries (Home <-> Work, Hotel <-> Airport, Office <-> Conference Center) across any city worldwide. Before you leave, the system performs an AI traffic scan that diagnoses the exact causes of congestion, discovers alternate routes standard apps crowd with traffic, checks weather and road friction, and dispatches a concise briefing message directly to your screen or messaging app.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Global everyday commuters, transit workers, delivery drivers, and busy families who want hassle-free departure timing without paying expensive software fees.
- **Key Value**: Delivers proactive certainty: knowing the best bypass route, why delays exist, and the exact departure minute to beat the rush.

### 3. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. *Quick GPS or Preset Route Setup*: User clicks "Use GPS Location" to autofill origin, selects destination, specifies scheduled departure/arrival, and sets alert window (e.g., 20 mins prior).
  2. *Global Hub & Route Selector*: Instant switching between global presets (e.g. Heathrow to Central London, JFK to Manhattan, Narita to Shibuya, Bandra to BKC Mumbai) or user-saved custom trips.
  3. *Executive Pre-Departure Dashboard*: Live ticking countdown clock (`font-mono tabular-nums`), weather advisory badge, and corridor status summary.
  4. *AI Traffic Dispatch & Radar Comparison*:
     - *Primary Route vs. AI Secret Bypass*: Visual comparative corridor telemetry showing distance, speed variance, and congestion tags.
     - *Chokepoint Root Cause Radar*: Breakdown of exact bottlenecks (e.g., "Lane narrowing at Mile 14", "Signal desync at Metro Junction").
     - *Corridor Weather Factor*: Rain/visibility delay coefficient.
     - *Departure Window Recommendation*: Optimal minute-by-minute leave window.
  5. *Simulated Message Dispatch & Audio Studio*:
     - *WhatsApp / Telegram / SMS Message Preview*: Styled chat bubble with instant copy, WhatsApp link, and Web Share sheet.
     - *Executive Audio Briefing*: Clear spoken briefing read aloud with playback controls and audio frequency visualization.
  6. *Fullscreen Departure Cockpit HUD*: High-contrast view designed for keeping on a stand or monitor while packing up.
  7. *Interactive Affordable Revenue & Volume Calculator*: Visual simulator to test $0.49 - $1.99 micro-pricing economics and global user base growth.

- **Visual Identity & Theme**:
  - *Aesthetic Direction*: Premium aeronautical and executive transit command center—deep slate canvas, refined typography, and high-precision data displays.
  - *Color Palette & Mood*:
    - Canvas Base: Deep Slate (`#0B1120`, `#0F172A`) with subtle 1px structural borders (`#1E293B`).
    - Flow Emerald (`#10B981`): Smooth corridor movement and optimal departure windows.
    - Advisory Amber (`#F59E0B`): Moderate choke and weather caution.
    - Congestion Crimson (`#EF4444`): Severe chokepoints and incidents.
    - Titanium & Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4` / `#3B82F6`): AI telemetry, GPS active signals, and audio waveforms.
  - *Typography & Hierarchy*:
    - Display Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans` for crisp executive structure.
    - Body Text: `DM Sans` for comfortable reading.
    - Telemetry & Countdown: Strict monospace `font-mono tabular-nums` for rock-solid vertical alignment.
  - *Anti-Slop Discipline*: No cheap pill badges, no arbitrary fake scoreboards, zero decorative pulsing dots on static text, and clean unboxed metadata with `·` separators.

- **Data Models**:
  - `PreplannedTrip`: id, title, origin, destination, scheduledDepartureTime, targetArrivalTime, daysOfWeek, alertLeadMinutes (e.g. 20m), preferredMode, units ('metric' | 'imperial'), notes.
  - `TrafficBriefing`: tripId, generatedAt, corridorSummary, estimatedTravelTimeMin, baselineTimeMin, delayMin, trafficLevel ('light' | 'moderate' | 'heavy' | 'severe'), weatherCondition: { temp, condition, delayImpact }, bottlenecks: `Array<{ location, cause, severity, delayContribution }>`, alternateBypasses: `Array<{ name, description, travelTimeMin, avoidsBottlenecks, pros, cons }>`, departureWindowRecommendation: `{ immediateLeaveAdvice, delayAdvice, bestWindowText }`, instantMessagePayload: string, voiceScript: string.
  - `MicroRevenueConfig`: monthlyPrice (default $0.99), annualPrice (default $7.99), activeCommuterVolume (default 25,000), targetMetros (default 5).
